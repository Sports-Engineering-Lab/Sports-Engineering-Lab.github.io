## Description
<!-- 본인에 대한 자세한 설명을 작성하세요. 연구 관심사, 학력, 업적, 현재 프로젝트 등을 자유롭게 포함할 수 있습니다. -->
Research Interests: Neuromuscular Control, Musculoskeletal Simulation, Biomechanics, Artificial Intelligence, Reinforcement Learning, Nonlinear Dynamics, Physics-Based Character Control, Human Pose Estimation

I am a master's student in the Sports Engineering Lab at Seoul National University, advised by Professor Jooeun Ahn.

My research goal is to build a digital twin of the human motor controller. Once this becomes possible, it would let us understand the principles of human motor control on a scientific level, and put that understanding to practical use in areas such as surgical support, rehabilitation, performance augmentation, and controller design for exoskeletons or any other system that physically interacts with the human body.

To get there, I believe a deep understanding of the human body has to come first. Beyond biomechanics, I actively study neuroscience and physiology, drawing on that knowledge and building it on top of state-of-the-art methods. My work ranges from classical mathematical modeling to reinforcement-learning-based musculoskeletal simulation, alongside hands-on biomechanics experiments, and I am also deeply interested in exoskeletons.
